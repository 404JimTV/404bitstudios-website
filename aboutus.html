Who We Are

404Bit Studios is an independent development studio focused on creating games, software tools, Discord bots, and digital experiences for players, creators, and online communities.

From small experimental projects to larger long-term goals, every project is built with creativity, learning, and community involvement at its core.

Our Mission

Our mission is to turn ideas into reality through games, software, and creative projects while building a welcoming community around development.

We believe every great project starts with a single line of code, a simple idea, and the determination to keep building.
