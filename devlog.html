Frankie Discord Bot

Devlog update #1 Date: 15 September 2026 Project Summary Frankie has received several major upgrades, expanding his automation abilities and improving community interaction within the 404JimTV Discord. These updates focus on content notifications, onboarding improvements, and role automation. Update Details YouTube RSS Integration Frankie now monitors the 404JimTV YouTube channel using an RSS feed. Whenever a new video or short is published, Frankie automatically posts it in Discord for viewers to see. Twitch Stream Detection Frankie now checks Twitch for live status updates. When James goes live, Frankie instantly announces the stream in Discord. Welcome System Improvements Frankie’s greeting system has been updated to provide a cleaner, more friendly welcome message to new members joining the server. Automatic Role Assignment The bot now assigns the Viewers role automatically to new members, ensuring they have access to the correct channels and content right away.
